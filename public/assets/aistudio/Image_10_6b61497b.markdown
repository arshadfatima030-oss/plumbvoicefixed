## Brand & Style
The design system positions itself at the intersection of rugged American craftsmanship and high-velocity Silicon Valley engineering. Designed exclusively for plumbing, electrical, and HVAC service business owners, it balances mission-critical operational reliability with bespoke warmth.

Key stylistic principles:
- **Craftsmanship & Utility:** Functional density inspired by modern high-efficiency developer tools (e.g., Linear), balanced with the human dignity and tangible materiality of trade tools (e.g., brushed copper, kiln clay, warm steel).
- **Absolute Trust & Clarity:** Zero visual gimmicks. Avoid excessive motion, frivolous gradients, or decorative noise. Metrics, dispatch statuses, call transcripts, and lead alerts must be legible at a glance from a field truck dashboard or a busy back-office dispatch desk.
- **Dignified Modernity:** A strict rejection of generic corporate SaaS blues. The visual tone relies on deep architectural charcoal, natural warm lime-washed paper backdrops, and metallic copper conduits.

## Colors
The palette evokes raw copper piping, electrical conduit warmth, and aged architectural blueprints while delivering stringent WCAG AA compliance across all touchpoints.

### Core Role Assignments
- **Primary / Brand Action (`#C8672B`):** Copper. Reserved for primary interactive triggers, active phone states, high-priority conversion flows, and decisive action links.
- **Secondary / Highlight (`#F2A93B`):** Warm Amber. Used for real-time live events, incoming call notifications, audio wave visualizations, and selective accent badges.
- **Dark Neutral / Structural Canvas (`#1C1F22`):** Deep Charcoal. Powers application navigation bars, primary headings, telephonic status headers, and high-impact structural framing.
- **Canvas & Backgrounds:**
  - Page Surface: `#FAF7F2` (Warm linen off-white; provides soft contrast against field truck screen glare).
  - Component Container: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for cards, panels, modal dialogs).
  - Container Border: `#ECE6DC` (Muted putty; establishes structural containment without visual heaviness).
- **Text Hierarchy:**
  - Headings & High-Emphasis: `#1C1F22`
  - Body & Form Values: `#23282D`
  - Secondary & Muted Context: `#7A746B`
  - Accent / Subtle Tint Fill: `#FBEBDD` (Soft copper wash for badge backgrounds and active table rows).
- **Semantic Feedback:**
  - Success / Call Booked: `#2E8B57` (Forest Green)
  - Warning / Missed or Escalated: `#E9A23B` (Warm Amber)
  - Danger / Emergency Pipe Burst / Lost Lead: `#D64545` (Crimson Red)

## Typography
Typography creates a strong architectural hierarchy. **Manrope** lends geometric structure and solid massing to headlines, call recording summaries, and quantitative telemetry. **Inter** ensures crystal-clear utility across multi-line live transcriptions, customer logs, and form inputs.

Guidelines:
- **Metric Displays (`metric-stat`):** Use for high-stakes KPIs (e.g., "99.4% Lead Capture", "12s Avg Pickup"). Metric labels must sit strictly beneath or adjacent using uppercase `label-caps`.
- **Micro-Tracking:** Apply `label-caps` in uppercase across table headers, category tags, caller status states, and section markers (`letter-spacing: 0.08em; text-transform: uppercase;`).
- **Call Transcripts:** Set phone caller dialogues in `body-md` with `lineHeight: 1.45rem` to prioritize readability during real-time speech processing.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a strict 12-column grid on desktop screens (1024px and up), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets (768px - 1023px), and a responsive single/two-column stacking flow on mobile (below 768px).

### Layout System
- **Maximum Width:** Content containers lock to a max structural width of `1280px` for operations dashboards, centering horizontally with fluid margins.
- **Sidebar Integration:** Desktop operations use a fixed `260px` structural charcoal sidebar (`#1C1F22`), with remaining space functioning as an auto-flowing grid with `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Space out metric clusters and primary tables using `space-xl` (40px). Group form inputs, call details, and transcript lines cleanly with `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px).

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics clean architectural layering: sheets of clean white paper and dark structural instruments set over a warm workshop plane. Ambient drop shadows are tinted subtly with charcoal and copper warmth rather than synthetic neutral grays.

### Shadow Scale
- **Flat Border (Surface Tier 0):** Zero elevation. Used on inner inset panels, table rows, and transcription speech bubbles. Boundaries are enforced with a `1px solid #ECE6DC` perimeter.
- **Card / Raised Tier (Tier 1):** `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(28, 31, 34, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(200, 103, 43, 0.03); border: 1px solid #ECE6DC;`
  Used across all standard dashboard lead cards, call summaries, and settings groups.
- **Floating / Active Card (Tier 2):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(28, 31, 34, 0.06), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(28, 31, 34, 0.08);`
  Applied on live-incoming call overlays, focused lead inspectors, and hover states.
- **Modal / Popover (Tier 3):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(28, 31, 34, 0.16), 0 1px 2px rgba(28, 31, 34, 0.06);`
  Used for direct customer call initiation panels and emergency dispatch dialogs.

## Shapes
The shape strategy combines structured rectangular containment with softened ergonomic touchpoints (`roundedness: 2` — 0.5rem base, with 12px / 0.75rem cards).

- **Cards & Data Surfaces:** Default to `12px` (`0.75rem`), balancing modern softness with maximum grid containment.
- **Interactive Buttons & Inputs:** Default to `8px` (`0.5rem`), providing clear click/tap affordance.
- **Status Badges & Live Indicators:** Strict pill shape (`9999px`) to immediately visually differentiate taxonomy tags from action buttons.
- **Border Treatment:** Always combine radius with a crisp `1px` structural outline (`#ECE6DC`) to retain clarity against light backgrounds.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Copper background (`#C8672B`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, `8px` radius, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`. On hover: `#B55A22`. On active/pressed: `#9E4D1A`. Subtle `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` shadow.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface white (`#FFFFFF`), charcoal text (`#1C1F22`), border `1px solid #ECE6DC`. On hover: background `#FAF7F2`, border `#DCD5C9`.
- **Emergency / Live Action Button:** Deep Charcoal (`#1C1F22`) with Amber highlight accents (`#F2A93B`), for overriding AI actions or jumping into ongoing phone calls.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Compact layout with `4px 10px` padding, uppercase `label-caps` typography, `9999px` radius.
  - *New Lead / Active Call:* Background `#FBEBDD`, Text `#C8672B`, border `1px solid rgba(200, 103, 43, 0.25)`.
  - *Booked Job:* Background `#EBF6F0`, Text `#2E8B57`, border `1px solid rgba(46, 139, 87, 0.25)`.
  - *Emergency Dispatch:* Background `#FDF0F0`, Text `#D64545`, border `1px solid rgba(214, 69, 69, 0.25)`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** Height `42px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #ECE6DC`, radius `8px`, typography `body-md` (`#23282D`).
- **Focus State:** Border changes to `#C8672B` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 103, 43, 0.15)` focus ring.
- **Input Labels:** `label-caps` in `#7A746B`, sitting strictly `6px` above the input control.

### Cards & Panels
- **Standard Lead / Metric Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #ECE6DC`, radius `12px`, padding `1.5rem`.
- **Card Header:** Separated from content by a structural rule or clear vertical spacing (`space-md`), featuring a category indicator in `label-caps` and primary values in `headline-md`.

### Call Transcripts & AI Audio Player
- **Dialogue Stream:** Distinct bubbles for Customer vs. Voice Receptionist:
  - *Voice Receptionist:* Background `#FAF7F2`, border `1px solid #ECE6DC`, aligned left.
  - *Customer:* Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #ECE6DC`, subtle ambient shadow.
- **Waveform / Audio Bar:** Deep Charcoal track `#1C1F22` with active playback scrub represented in metallic Copper (`#C8672B`) and live speech indicators in Amber (`#F2A93B`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `18px x 18px`, border `1.5px solid #7A746B`, radius `4px` (checkbox) or `9999px` (radio). Checked state: fill `#C8672B` with white checkmark glyph.
